# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TinyCore-8 is an educational open-source project for people learning digital electronics, Verilog HDL, RTL design, and computer architecture. We want the project to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain technical feedback clearly.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share reproducible technical information when reporting problems.
- Respect different viewpoints and learning speeds.
- Give credit for work and ideas that are not your own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, humiliating comments, or deliberately hostile responses.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or other disruptive behaviour.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour in project discussions, issues, or pull requests, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile:

https://github.com/chetanrmaradi

Please include relevant links or context. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect a respectful learning environment.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.
